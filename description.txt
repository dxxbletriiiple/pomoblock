Extension Name
PomoBlock

Short Description
Stay focused with Pomodoro sessions that automatically block distracting websites and unblock them on breaks.

Detailed Description
PomoBlock helps you stay in deep focus by combining a Pomodoro timer with automatic website blocking.

Start a focus session, and your selected distracting sites are blocked instantly, including tabs that are already open.
When your work session ends, those sites are unblocked automatically so you can take your break without friction.

What you can do with PomoBlock:
- Run focus, short break, and long break Pomodoro cycles
- Customize work/break durations and cycle count
- Build and manage your personal block list
- Automatically block/unblock sites based on timer phase
- Keep timer state persistent even if popup/service worker restarts
- Use the extension in English, Russian, or Spanish
- Switch between light and dark themes

Designed for:
- Students
- Developers
- Remote workers
- Anyone who wants fewer distractions and better focus

Privacy:
PomoBlock works locally in your browser.
No accounts, no tracking, no remote analytics.
